## 1.2.0

- Draco Ambush has been reworked
  - Base damage reduced from 10000% to 7000%
  - Confirming an attack with Draco Ambush now starts a short minigame: "Elemental Attunement"
    - Press the primary button when the elemental rings shrink to overlap with the center ring. Hitting specific rings grants bonus effects, such as increased explosion damage or decreased skill cooldown. Missing a hit decrements from all currently acquired bonuses (but never worsens the attack beyond its base stats). Letting an elemental ring pass does not count as a miss, and will not decrement your current bonuses
  - Aiming the skill now displays the keybinds for confirm and cancel as a UI element (toggleable in the config)
  - Confirming and cancelling aiming are now handled as skill overrides, and have unique skill icons, names, and descriptions
<br></br>
- Burst Thrusters now has custom implementation to try and interact correctly with Alien Head, Eclipse Lite, and Hardlight Afterburner

- Downward projectile attacks now have a bonus 25% vertical recoil

- Fixed the spawn animation camera rotation not aligning with the landing rotation

- Fixed some potential issues when trying to use Draco Ambush while Burst Thrusters is active

- Fixed the Burst Thrusters animation being slightly off-center
